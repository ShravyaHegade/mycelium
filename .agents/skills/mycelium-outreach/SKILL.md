# Mycelium Outreach

Write the outreach as a personal note from the user, not as a campaign or a
request for unpaid work.

## Required message

Preserve this intent:

- Explain that the sender found the recipient through a specific GitHub issue,
  discussion, report, or comment. Name and link it when supplied.
- Invite the recipient to try Mycelium and link the repository when available.
- Ask them to open an issue or share candid feedback if Mycelium is missing
  something relevant to their experience.
- Make participation optional and low-pressure.

Do not imply that the recipient is assigned to Mycelium, expected to fix the
issue where they were found, or already interested in contributing. Do not turn
the note into a follow-up about solving that original issue unless the user asks
for that specifically.

## Drafting

When a linked issue or discussion is available, inspect enough context to state
truthfully whether the recipient reported it, commented on it, or contributed
to it. Do not invent their real name, role, relationship to the project, or a
connection between Mycelium and the issue.

Keep the copy concise, direct, and human. Avoid inflated product claims,
flattery, corporate language, and detailed technical prescriptions. Mention the
recipient's relevant experience in one sentence, then make the invitation.

For email, include a plain subject and body. For a DM or chat message, omit the
subject. Match the user's requested tone and return copy-pasteable text without
commentary unless they ask for alternatives or explanation.

Do not send the message or look up private contact details unless the user
separately requests and authorizes that action.
